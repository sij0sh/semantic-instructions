**Core:** Instructions are guilty of redundancy until they demonstrate unique value.

**Assume model competence.** Don't explain established coding practices. Instruct the agent on what is unique to the task, constraints, or desired reasoning.

**Describe outcomes, not procedures.** Define the problem, objective, and completion condition. Avoid prescribing implementation steps unless their order or method matters.

**One instruction, one responsibility.** Each semantic instruction should target a distinct behavior or failure mode. Avoid mixing planning, implementation, evaluation, and reporting into one prompt.
